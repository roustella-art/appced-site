Atelier Guitare Support

Atelier Guitare is a guitar app for iPhone: a tuner, a metronome and exercises to learn the fretboard, designed by a guitar teacher. This page answers the most common questions. For anything else, write to me.

Contact

Write to contact@appced.com. Please include your iPhone model, your iOS version and what happens. I answer myself, usually within a few days.

Frequently asked questions

Is the app free?

The tuner, the metronome and the first exercises of each family are free, with no account. All the exercises are available with Atelier Guitare Pro, a monthly or yearly subscription.

How do I subscribe?

Tap any locked exercise (padlock), or open the app's settings, then choose a plan. Payment is handled by Apple.

I already subscribed, but the exercises are still locked

Open the subscription screen and tap Restore, at the top right. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy, and that you have an internet connection. If nothing changes, write to me with the purchase date.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Cancellation is done with Apple, not in the app: iPhone Settings › your name › Subscriptions › Atelier Guitare Pro › Cancel. Cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period to avoid renewal.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are decided by Apple. You can request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

The tuner or the "Guitar" mode can't hear me

The app needs microphone access. Check in Settings › Atelier Guitare that the microphone is allowed. Hold the iPhone close to the guitar, in a quiet place, and play one string at a time.

Can I answer with my voice?

Yes, it is an option, off by default, that you turn on in the app's settings. The app only listens while you hold a button.

My data and my progress

There is no account: your scores, settings and progress stay on your iPhone and are not sent anywhere. If you delete the app, they are deleted. Details are in the privacy policy.

Does the app work offline?

Yes. A connection is only needed to subscribe or restore a purchase.

Is the app available in French?

Yes, in French and in English. Cette page existe aussi en français.
