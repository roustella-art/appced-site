Atelier Guitare Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026. Version française

Atelier Guitare is a guitar learning app: a tuner, a metronome and exercises to learn the fretboard. This page explains plainly what the app does with your data, and above all what it does not do.

No personal data is collected

Atelier Guitare does not collect, transmit or store any personal data on a server. The app contains no analytics tools and no advertising trackers, and shares no information with third parties. There is no account to create: the app never asks for your name, your email address or anything else about you.

The microphone

The app asks for microphone access for two uses: the tuner, and the "Guitar" mode of the exercises, where you play the requested note on your instrument and the app checks that it is the right one.

The sound picked up is analyzed live on your device, then immediately discarded. The app measures the pitch of the sound to work out a note; it records nothing, keeps no audio file, and sends no sound anywhere. Nothing the microphone hears leaves your device.

The microphone is only active while the tuner is on screen, or during an exercise in "Guitar" mode. You can withdraw this permission at any time in iOS Settings; the tuner and the "Guitar" mode then stop working, and the rest of the app keeps working normally.

Speech recognition

If you turn it on, the exercises let you answer by saying the name of the note out loud instead of typing it. This feature is off by default and must be switched on explicitly in the app's settings.

It uses Apple's speech recognition. The app requests on-device processing whenever the device is capable of it, in which case nothing is transmitted. On older devices that are not capable of it, Apple performs the recognition on its servers: in that case, what you say while the app is listening is sent to Apple and handled under Apple's privacy policy. For its part, Atelier Guitare only receives the recognized text, which it uses immediately to check your answer and does not keep.

By default, listening only happens while you hold a button, not continuously. This is a deliberate choice, so that a microphone is never left open in a classroom without being asked.

What is stored, and where

All data created by using the app is saved only on your device: your settings (string colors, fretboard display, metronome tempo and sound), your exercise scores, and the working memory that remembers which notes you struggle with so that they come back. This data never leaves your device, is never sent to Cédric or to any third party, and is deleted if you uninstall the app or clear its data. The working memory can also be reset at any time from the app's settings.

Offline use

The app works entirely without an internet connection. It makes no network requests of its own accord. A connection is only needed to subscribe or to restore a subscription, operations handled by the App Store.

Subscription and payment

The tuner and the metronome are free and unlimited. The full exercises are offered by subscription. The subscription renews automatically unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period, and can be managed from Settings › your name › Subscriptions on your device.

Payment is handled entirely by Apple (App Store). Atelier Guitare does not receive, see or store any banking or payment information: this is entirely delegated to Apple, under its own privacy policy. The app only knows whether a subscription is active or not.

Children

The app is used by students of all ages, including minors. It collects no personal data, shows no advertising, has no content generated by other users and no way to communicate with third parties. A minor can therefore use it without any information about them being collected.

Your rights

Since no personal data is collected or transmitted, there is no data about you for us to give you access to, correct, export or delete. The data kept on your device is under your control: you erase it by uninstalling the app or clearing its data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the last-updated date at the top of this page will be changed. Significant changes will be mentioned in the app's release notes.
